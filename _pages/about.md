I'm Olaf [u:lɑf], a postdoctoral researcher at University of South-Eastern Norway. I am a quantitative linguist working within usage-based framework and I use corpus linguistic, computational and experimental methods to answer questions about language. Having spent most of my adult life abroad learning and teaching languages and linguistics, I am now back in my native Norway as part of the [Centre for the Use of Norwegian as an Academic Language](https://www.hf.uio.no/senter-for-norsk-fagsprak-i-bruk/english/), which seeks to expand the knowledge base and preserve the use of Norwegian as a professional language in research, education, and public administration.
